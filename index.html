About Rooikat Labs

Business Experience
Meets AI Technology

Rooikat Labs was built on a simple belief: the best technology solutions come from people who understand business, not just code. We bring decades of real-world business experience to every project — so we don't just build what you ask for, we build what you actually need.

Our name comes from the Afrikaans word for the caracal — the sharp-eyed, agile wildcat of Africa. Precise. Alert. Built for results. That's how we approach every project.
