Relationships need
intentional care.

Roots is a private relationship management app grounded in Dunbar number theory — the science of how many meaningful connections a person can realistically maintain. It helps you stay genuinely close to the people who matter most, without the noise of social media.

  • Circle layers — organise your relationships by closeness: Intimate, Close, Active, and Meaningful
  • Connection health scoring — a decay model that surfaces who you haven't reached out to in a while
  • Smart nudges — push notifications and WhatsApp reminders timed to your contact frequency preferences
  • Shared memories — capture moments together with photos, voice notes, and multiple perspectives
  • Contacts sync — links your phone contacts to your circle automatically
