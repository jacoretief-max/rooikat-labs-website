Common questions

I didn't receive my verification code

Enter your number in international format, starting with + and your country code (for example +1 303 555 0123). Codes can take up to a minute to arrive. You can tap Resend code after 30 seconds.

If you previously replied STOP to one of our messages, reply START to the same number to receive codes again. If it still doesn't work, email us with your country and phone carrier.

I forgot my password

Email info@rooikatlabs.com from the email address on your account and we'll help you regain access. If you're signed in, you can change your password under Profile → Change password.

How do I download a copy of my data?

Go to Profile → Privacy & Data → Export my data. You'll get a file with your memories, photos and circle, including a page you can open in any web browser.

How do I delete my account?

Go to Profile → Privacy & Data → Delete account. You'll be offered an export first, then asked for your password. Deletion is permanent. See our Privacy Policy for what happens to your data.

How do I report something or block someone?

Tap the ⚑ flag on a photo or perspective, use the ••• menu on a memory, or open a person's profile in your circle and tap Report or Block. Reported content is hidden from you straight away and reviewed by us. You can manage blocked people under Profile → Blocked people.

If a child may be at risk, report it in the app as Child safety. If a child is in immediate danger, contact your local emergency services, or report to NCMEC's CyberTipline.

Why is my photo "under review"?

Photos are checked automatically when uploaded, and videos are reviewed by a person. While a photo or video is under review, only you can see it. Most reviews are completed within a day.

Who can see my memories?

Only the people you tag in a memory and the people in the circle layer you choose for it. There are no public posts, no ads, and we never sell your data.

My account was suspended

Accounts can be suspended while we review a report. If you think this was a mistake, email info@rooikatlabs.com from the email address on your account.
