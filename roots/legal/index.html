1. Privacy Policy

Rooted In is a private app for keeping in touch with the people who matter most. There are no ads, no public posts, and we never sell your data. This policy explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have.

What we store

  • Account details: your display name, email address, date of birth, mobile phone number, city, avatar colour and optional bio.
  • Your content: the memories, perspectives and photos you add, and the people you tag in them.
  • Your circle: your connections, the relation types and Dunbar layers you assign, and any contact details you choose to save for people in your circle.
  • Verification records: when you ask for a verification code, we record the phone number, the time, the consent wording you agreed to, your IP address and device type. This is evidence of your consent to receive the text message.
  • Technical data: the minimum needed to run the app, such as sign-in tokens and a push notification token for your device.

Why we use your phone number

We use your phone number to confirm you are a real person when you sign up or change your number, which helps keep bots and fake accounts out of Rooted In. Your number also helps match you with people already in your circle. We do not use your number for marketing.

Who can see your memories

Only the people you explicitly tag in a memory, plus anyone within the Dunbar layer you set for that memory. No one outside your circle can see anything you post. There are no public posts on Rooted In.

Contacts and calendar

If you choose to connect your phone contacts or calendar, we read only what is needed to suggest matches and log moments with people in your circle. You decide who is added to your circle; nothing is imported automatically. You can turn these permissions off at any time in your device settings.

Photos and media

Photos you upload are stored securely in encrypted cloud storage and are only accessible to people within the visibility layer you set for that memory. Photos are never used for advertising or shared with third parties.

Cookies and tracking

Rooted In uses only what is essential to keep you signed in. We do not use advertising cookies, tracking cookies or third-party analytics, and your usage data is never sold or shared with advertisers.

Service providers

We use a small number of trusted providers to run Rooted In. They process data only on our behalf:

  • Twilio — sends SMS verification codes to your phone number.
  • Railway — hosts our servers and database.
  • Amazon Web Services — stores photos you upload.
  • Expo — delivers push notifications to your device.

Retention and deletion

You can delete your account at any time from the app. Your account data, memories and photos are permanently removed within 30 days. SMS consent records (phone number, time and consent wording) are kept after deletion because carriers and our SMS provider require us to be able to prove consent; they are not linked to your account once it is deleted.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of your data, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it by emailing info@rooikatlabs.com. Depending on where you live (for example under GDPR in the EU or POPIA in South Africa), you may have additional rights, and we will honour them.

Age

Rooted In is for adults aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18, and we remove such accounts when we become aware of them.

Changes to this policy

If we make material changes we will update the date above and let you know in the app.

2. SMS Terms (verification codes)

Rooted In sends one-time verification codes by text message when you sign up or change your phone number. By providing your phone number and opting in, you agree to these terms.

  • What you receive: by providing your phone number and opting into our text messaging program, you consent to receive a one-time transactional security code on your mobile device. We send codes only when you request one. We do not send marketing messages.
  • Frequency: one message per code you request.
  • Cost: Standard message and data rates may apply.
  • Help: for assistance, reply HELP to the number from which you received the message, or contact us at info@rooikatlabs.com.
  • Opt out: to stop receiving messages, reply STOP at any time. Note that you will not be able to receive verification codes, and therefore cannot sign up or change your number, while opted out.
  • Carriers: carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

Your phone number and consent records are handled as described in the Privacy Policy above.
